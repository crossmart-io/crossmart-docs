title: Channels

A channel is a marketplace or store platform you sell on. Today you can sell on Shopify and eBay;
Google Shopping and Etsy are coming soon. You connect one or more stores of a channel, and
Crossmart publishes your catalog there and keeps it in step. See
[Connect a store](/sellers/connect-a-store/) for the steps.

| Channel                                       | Status      | Publish | Prices | Stock | Orders | Import catalog |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------- | :-----: | :----: | :---: | :----: | :------------: |
| [Shopify](/channels/shopify/)                 | Live        |   Yes   |  Yes   |  Yes  |  Yes   |      Yes       |
| [eBay](/channels/ebay/)                       | Live        |   Yes   |  Yes   |  Yes  |  Yes   |       No       |
| [Google Shopping](/channels/google-shopping/) | Coming soon |   Yes   |  Yes   |  No   |   No   |       No       |
| [Etsy](/channels/etsy/)                       | Coming soon |   Yes   |  Yes   |  Yes  |  Yes   |       No       |

For channels that are coming soon, the table shows what the connector is built to do. Google
Shopping gets each product's availability (in or out of stock) when it is published or updated,
not every stock change. Amazon, Walmart and TikTok Shop are planned. The **Stores** page in the
dashboard lists the channels you can connect a store on today; to ask for another, click
**Request a marketplace** there.
