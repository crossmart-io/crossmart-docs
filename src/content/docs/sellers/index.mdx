Crossmart keeps one catalog and one stock ledger for your business, and publishes them to every
store you connect. You add a product once, choose the stores it sells on, and Crossmart creates
and updates the listings there. When an order arrives from any store, its stock is held at once
and every other store gets the new quantity, so you don't sell what you no longer have.

Everything you do in the dashboard, at [app.crossmart.io](https://app.crossmart.io), can also be
done by an [AI agent](/agents/) or through the [REST API](/developers/).

## The words Crossmart uses

| Word             | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Workspace**    | One business, with its own catalog, stock, stores, orders and members. An organization (your account) can hold several.                                                        |
| **Product**      | A catalog item with a title, description, images and tags, and one or more **variants** (such as sizes or colours), each with its own SKU, price and stock.                    |
| **Channel**      | A place you sell, such as Shopify or eBay. See [Channels](/channels/).                                                                                                         |
| **Store**        | One connected account on a channel: a Shopify store, or an eBay account on one marketplace. You can connect several stores of the same channel.                                |
| **Listing**      | A product on one store. Each listing has a [state](/sellers/listing-states/) and can show the product [differently in that store](/sellers/listing-editor/).                   |
| **Pricing rule** | How a store prices every product: a markup or discount, rounding, a floor. See [Pricing](/sellers/pricing/).                                                                   |
| **Suggestion**   | A change proposed by Crossmart's AI, such as a new title for one store. Nothing changes until a person approves it under **Agent → Review**.                                   |
| **Proposal**     | A change an AI agent you connected wants to make. It waits under **Agent → Review** until you approve it, unless you let agents apply changes directly. New drafts don't wait. |

## First steps

<Steps>

1. **Connect a store.** Open **Stores**, pick the channel and click **Connect**. You approve
   Crossmart on the channel's own site, then one setup step asks for what the store needs (such
   as eBay's business policies) and offers to add all your active products. See
   [Connect a store](/sellers/connect-a-store/).

2. **Add your products.** Click **Add product** on **Products**, choose the stores under
   **Publish to**, and click **Save & publish**. See [Products](/sellers/products/). You can
   also **Import** a CSV file on **Products**, or import the catalog of a connected Shopify
   store.

3. **Publish the rest.** Add products to a store from the product's page, from the products
   table, or with the store's automatic rule. See [Publish products](/sellers/publish-products/).

4. **Check what needs you.** **Home** opens with **Needs attention**: stores to reconnect,
   listings a store refused, and changes waiting for your review. See
   [Home](/sellers/home/).

5. **Mark one-off items sold.** When an item sells somewhere Crossmart doesn't manage, **Mark as
   sold** takes it off every store at once. See [Mark as sold](/sellers/products/#mark-as-sold).

</Steps>

## Find your way around

The sidebar has six items:

| Item         | What's there                                                                                                           |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Home**     | **Needs attention**, your first steps while setup is unfinished, and sales. **Activity** opens the log                 |
| **Products** | Your catalog. The **Stock** tab has every variant's stock; **Import** brings in a CSV file                             |
| **Orders**   | Orders from every store                                                                                                |
| **Stores**   | Your connected stores, each with its listings and settings, and **Connect** for a new one                              |
| **Agent**    | [Crossmart Agent](/sellers/crossmart-agent/) and its map, and **Review** for changes waiting for you                   |
| **Settings** | Tabs: **General**, **Team** (members and roles), **Billing** and **Developers** (API keys, webhooks, connected agents) |

Your own account, including deleting it, is under **Account** in the menu behind your avatar.
Bookmarks to older pages, such as `/channels` or `/settings/api-keys`, still open the right place.

## Who can do what

Every member of a workspace has a role. Owners and Admins connect stores and set pricing rules;
Editors manage products and stock and publish them; Approvers review AI suggestions; Viewers can
look but not change anything. See [Roles and permissions](/developers/permissions/) for the full
list.

## Plans

Plans differ by the number of active listings, connected stores and other limits. See the plans
at [crossmart.io/#pricing](https://crossmart.io/#pricing). Your current plan and usage are under
**Settings → Billing**.
