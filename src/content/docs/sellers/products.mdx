Your catalog is under **Products**. Each product has a page with its photos, text, price,
condition and stock, and a **Stores** card that shows where it is listed.

## Add a product and publish it

<Steps>

1. On **Products**, click **Add product**. A new product starts **Active** with a quantity of 1.

2. Add photos, a title and a description, then the price, the [condition](#item-condition) and
   the quantity.

3. Under **Publish to**, tick the stores to sell it on. Every store that is ready is ticked
   already.

4. Click **Save & publish**. The product is saved and added to each ticked store at once.

</Steps>

**Save as draft** saves the product as a draft and adds it nowhere. With no store ticked, the
main button just says **Save**.

In **Publish to**:

- A store that needs reconnecting can't be ticked; **Reconnect first** takes you there.
- A store whose [automatic rule](/sellers/publish-products/#add-products-to-a-store) takes every
  active product is ticked and locked (**Adds every active product**).
- The card shows only for roles that can publish listings (`listings:edit`).

If a store doesn't take the product, the product page opens with a note such as **Added to 1 of
2 stores**, with the store's reason. The product is saved either way; add it to that store again
from the **Stores** card once the cause is fixed. When a store needs a detail first, such as an
eBay category, the product page opens with that store's listing so you can fill it in. See
[Listing states](/sellers/listing-states/).

## The product page

The main column has, in this order:

- **Photos.** The first one is the main image.
- **Title** and **Description**. **Write with AI**, next to the title, drafts both; you can give
  it an instruction or pick a quick one, such as **Shorter**. Nothing is saved until you save.
- **Price**, **Condition** and **Quantity**.
- **More details**, folded: compare-at price, cost, SKU, barcode, stock tracking, tax, shipping,
  type, brand or vendor, tags, and variants. It opens by itself for a product with variants.

The side column has the product's status and the **Stores** card: each store with the product's
state there, **Add to store** or **Edit listing**, and the fix when something is missing.
**View map** opens the map on the [Crossmart Agent](/sellers/crossmart-agent/) page. Below
them, **Recent activity** lists the product's latest changes, and **All activity** opens the
workspace's activity log.

## Item condition

**Condition** says what state the item is in. Choose one of:

**New** · **New (other)** · **New with defects** · **Like new** · **Refurbished** ·
**Used – excellent** · **Used – very good** · **Used – good** · **Used – fair** ·
**For parts / not working**

Each store gets it in its own terms:

| Channel         | What it receives                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| eBay            | The eBay grade of the same name; **Used – fair** is sent as Used – acceptable, **For parts / not working** as For parts or not working. See [eBay: item condition](/channels/ebay/#item-condition) |
| Google Shopping | **New** and **Refurbished** as they are; every other condition as used                                                                                                                             |
| Shopify, Etsy   | Not sent: neither has a condition field                                                                                                                                                            |

**Not set** leaves it to each store: eBay uses the condition in the store's
[listing defaults](/channels/ebay/#listing-defaults), and Google Shopping sends new.

Some eBay categories don't take every grade. eBay then refuses the listing with its own message;
choose another condition and the listing is sent again.

## Mark as sold

When a one-of-a-kind item sells somewhere Crossmart doesn't manage, such as at a market stall,
**Mark as sold** makes sure it can't sell twice. It is on the product page's header and in the
row menu (**⋯**) on **Products**.

The confirmation names every store the item comes off. Confirming:

- sets the available stock of every tracked variant to 0, recorded with the reason **Sold**
  (units held for open orders stay held);
- takes the product off every connected store that lists it, or whose automatic rule would add
  it, the same as [removing it from a store](/sellers/publish-products/#remove-a-product-from-a-store),
  so the rule doesn't add it back.

The product stays in your catalog with its status unchanged. Marking it sold again changes
nothing (**Already sold. Nothing changed.**), and a product with no stock left that is on no
store shows **Sold** instead of the button. To sell it again, set its stock and add it back to
its stores.

<Aside>
  Marking a product sold needs permission to edit products. AI agents can propose it with the MCP
  tool `mark_product_sold` (it waits for your [approval](/sellers/agent-proposals/), like every
  change except adding a new draft), and programs can call `POST /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}
  /products/{productId}
  /mark-sold`.
</Aside>
