Crossmart Agent is an AI agent you chat with inside Crossmart. You ask it to do something, such
as "Fix my failed eBay listings", and it looks up what it needs and proposes the changes. Nothing
changes until you approve. While it works, you see on a live map of your catalog and stores what
it is reading and changing, next to a stream of everything that happens in your workspace.

It works inside Crossmart, so no assistant of your own is needed. Outside agents you
[connect](/agents/) use the same tools and limits.

<Aside>
  Crossmart Agent was called Agent Worker. The API and the `agent_worker:use` permission keep that
  name.
</Aside>

## Open it

- **The sidebar:** **Crossmart Agent**, where **Channel map** used to be. Old links to the
  channel map open this page.
- **Anywhere:** the **Crossmart Agent** button in the header, or <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>J</kbd>
  (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>J</kbd> on Windows and Linux).
- **A store with failed listings:** **Fix with Crossmart Agent** on the store's card opens the page
  with a message ready to send ("Fix the failed listings on …"). Edit it or press send.
- **A product's page:** the product's **Channel map** card has **Open in Crossmart Agent**. There,
  "this product" means that product.

A conversation keeps running while you visit other pages; come back to see where it is.

## The page

The page has three parts side by side. On a phone they are three tabs: **Chat**, **Map** and
**Events**.

### Chat

Your conversation with Crossmart Agent. Earlier conversations are under **Conversations**, and the
pen button starts a new one. Only you see your conversations. Each step the agent takes shows as a
short line, such as "List a store's products by state · 3 found", and changes it proposes wait in a
**Review the changes** card (see below).

### Map

Your catalog and every store it feeds, with how many products are published, pending, failed or
not listed on each. Above it, a status bar shows at a glance:

- **Stores:** how many are connected with no failed listings, of all your stores.
- **Listings:** how many are live, and how many failed.
- **Agent:** idle, working, or waiting for your approval.
- **Last sync:** when a store last synced.

The map shows what Crossmart Agent is doing as it does it:

- The store or the catalog it is working on is outlined, with a label: "Reading: List a store's
  products by state", "Proposed: 3 changes", "Updating: …", "Applied: …".
- A number on a store counts the changes waiting for you there. Click it to go to them in the chat.
- While an approved change is on its way to a store, the line to that store moves. The counts
  update as the store's listings are published or fail.

### Events

A live timeline of what happens in your workspace, newest first:

- Crossmart Agent's steps: what it read, what it proposed, what you approved or rejected, and what
  was applied or failed.
- Other agents and programs: changes made through MCP or an API key, named after the agent or key.
- Listings published or refused, with the marketplace's reason.
- Stock: levels a store refused, and corrections made by the regular stock check.
- Orders as they arrive.
- Webhook deliveries that failed.

Filter it with **All**, **Agent**, **Stores**, **Orders** or **Errors**. Point at an event to
outline its store or product on the map; click it to open what it is about (a product, an order,
a store, a webhook, or the changes waiting in the chat). On a phone, the **Events** tab counts the
events you haven't seen, and the **Map** tab lights up while the agent is working.

Which events you see depends on your role: orders need permission to view orders, webhook
failures need permission to manage webhooks, and only your own conversations show agent steps.
Other people's approved changes still show, as changes by Crossmart Agent on their behalf.

## What it can do

Crossmart Agent works with your catalog, stock, stores and orders. For example:

- "Which products are low on stock?"
- "Fix my failed eBay listings"
- "Publish the Linen Shirt to my Outlet store"
- "Rewrite titles for Etsy"
- "Add 20 units of the M size, stock received"
- "Change the price of the M size to $43"

It can find products, stock, listings, orders and reports, edit products and prices, adjust
stock, add products to stores and take them off, and ask Crossmart's AI for content. It can also
answer questions about Crossmart itself.

It can't connect or disconnect stores, manage members or API keys, change settings or billing, or
manage webhooks. Do those yourself in the dashboard.

## Reads run at once, changes wait for you

When Crossmart Agent only needs to look something up, it does so right away.

When it wants to change something, it stops and shows a **Review the changes** card. Each change
says what it does in plain words, which product and store it touches, and the values **Now** and
**After**, such as "Price (M): $48.00 → $43.00". Longer text, such as a description, shows which
words change.

- Tick the changes you want and click **Approve selected**. The ones you leave unticked are
  rejected.
- **Reject all** turns them all down.
- If you send a new message instead, the changes still waiting are dropped.

Approved changes run with your permissions at that moment, and each one shows whether it was
applied or failed. Crossmart Agent then carries on with the results. Every change is recorded in
**Activity** as "Crossmart Agent" on your behalf.

**Stop** ends what Crossmart Agent is doing.

## Extra confirmation for risky changes

Some changes carry a red badge and need a second step. Before **Approve selected** works, you
tick a box such as "I understand this deletes".

| Badge              | When                                                                                  |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Deletes**        | The change deletes something or takes it off sale                                     |
| **Changes prices** | The change sets a price or compare-at price that differs from today's                 |
| **Many items**     | The change touches more than 5 items, or your approval publishes more than 5 listings |

If text Crossmart Agent read in that step looks like instructions to an AI (for example, a product
description that says "ignore previous instructions"), the card warns you: "Text read in this
turn looked like instructions; review carefully". Crossmart Agent treats what it reads as data,
and it can still only propose; you decide.

## AI credits

Every time Crossmart Agent calls the AI model, it uses one AI credit, the same as Crossmart's
other AI features. One message can take several calls, because it calls the model again after
each lookup. A message makes at most 8 calls.

- If you have no credits left, Crossmart Agent can't start.
- If credits run out partway through, it stops and keeps what it already did.

The map and the events don't use AI credits. Your credits are under **Settings → Plan & usage**.

## Who can use it

Owners, Admins and Editors can chat with Crossmart Agent. Approvers and Viewers can open the page
and follow the map and the events, but can't chat. A custom role can include the chat with the
`agent_worker:use` permission (see [Roles and permissions](/developers/permissions/)); the map and
events need permission to view activity.

Crossmart Agent can never do more than your role allows. If your role can't make a change, the
change is refused when you approve it, and the card says so.

## When it is limited

- **A sandbox account** (one an AI agent created for you that you haven't claimed yet) can't chat
  with Crossmart Agent. Claim the account first.
- **A read-only account** (when the subscription isn't paid) can still chat with Crossmart Agent
  and look things up, but it can't propose changes.
- **One message at a time** per conversation. Wait for the answer, or press **Stop**.

Conversations are kept for 180 days after the last message, then deleted.

<Aside title="For developers">
  Crossmart Agent uses the same tools as outside agents ([developer reference](/developers/mcp/)),
  and every call goes through the same API, with the same checks and the same activity log. It never
  makes a change on its own: every change waits for your approval. The events on the page
  come from the workspace event stream, `GET /workspaces/{workspaceId}/events/stream` (server-sent
  events, resumable with `Last-Event-ID`), or `GET /workspaces/{workspaceId}/events?after=…` to
  poll. Both need the `activity:view` permission.
</Aside>
