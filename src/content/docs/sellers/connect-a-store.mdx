A store is one account on a channel: a Shopify store, or an eBay account on one marketplace.
Crossmart never asks for your marketplace password. You sign in on the channel's own site and
approve Crossmart there, and the channel sends you back.

Connecting a store needs the **Owner** or **Admin** role, and your plan sets how many stores you
can connect.

## Connect

<Steps>

1. Open **Stores** in the dashboard and pick the channel.

2. Click **Connect** and name the account:
   - **Shopify:** your store's domain, the `your-store` part of `your-store.myshopify.com`. Find
     it in your Shopify admin under **Settings → Domains**. Your own domain won't work here.
   - **eBay:** the marketplace you sell on, such as eBay US or eBay UK. Connect each marketplace
     separately; you sign in with the same eBay account.

3. Sign in on the channel and approve Crossmart. You come back to **Stores** in Crossmart.

4. Finish the one setup step (below), or click **Skip** to do it later.

</Steps>

Each channel's page has the details: [Shopify](/channels/shopify/), [eBay](/channels/ebay/).
[Google Shopping](/channels/google-shopping/) and [Etsy](/channels/etsy/) are coming soon. To
ask for another marketplace, click **Request a marketplace** on **Stores**.

## The setup step

Right after you connect a store, a **Set up** dialog asks for what the store needs before it can
publish, and nothing else:

- **eBay:** a shipping, payment and return policy, and where items ship from. When the account has
  no policies yet, **Create default policies** makes them for you
  ([details](/channels/ebay/#no-policies-yet)).
- **Shopify:** nothing.

**Add all my active products** is on by default. It turns on the store's
[automatic rule](/sellers/publish-products/#add-products-to-a-store) for every active product, so
products you make active later are added too. Untick it to choose products yourself.

**Save** does both. **Skip** leaves the store as it is; you can do the same later in the store's
**Settings** tab. The dialog shows only to Owners and Admins, and only for a store connected in
the last few minutes, so reconnecting a store doesn't ask again.

## Bring your products in

- **From a Shopify store:** on a connected Shopify store, click **Import products from Shopify**.
  Products already in your catalog (same handle or SKU) are linked, not copied, and new ones are
  created with their stock. Running the import again is safe. See
  [Shopify: importing your catalog](/channels/shopify/#import-your-catalog).
- **From a file:** on **Products**, click **Import** and upload a Shopify product export (CSV).
  Products whose handle is already in your catalog are skipped, and rows with problems are listed
  with their row number. A two-column `sku,available` file updates stock. **Import history**, in
  the same dialog, lists earlier imports.
- **By hand:** **Products → Add product**. See [Products](/sellers/products/).

## Finish the store's setup

Some channels need details before anything can be published. If you skipped the setup step,
the store's page shows **Finish setup to publish** with the list of what is missing, and products
added to the store wait in [Needs details](/sellers/listing-states/). Fill them in on the store's
**Settings** tab and click **Save** in the bar at the bottom.

| Channel         | What the setup asks for                                                                                                                    |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Shopify         | Nothing                                                                                                                                    |
| eBay            | A shipping, payment and return policy, and where items ship from, under **Listing defaults** ([details](/channels/ebay/#listing-defaults)) |
| Google Shopping | The country and the product page address, under **Listing setup**                                                                          |
| Etsy            | Who made the items, when, whether they're supplies, and a shipping and processing profile                                                  |

## Reconnect a store

A channel can stop accepting Crossmart's access, for example when you uninstall the app or the
sign-in expires. The store then shows **Reconnect this store to keep it in sync**, it appears
under **Needs attention** on [Home](/sellers/home/), and nothing reaches it until you click
**Reconnect** and approve Crossmart again. Your products, listings and settings are kept.

Shopify may also show **This store needs new permissions** when a feature, such as showing
products in your Online Store or reading orders, needs an approval you haven't given yet. Click
**Approve in Shopify** once; nothing else changes.

## Disconnect a store

**Disconnect** in the store's page stops all syncing to it and deletes the access Crossmart holds.
Your products stay in your catalog.

<Aside>
  Your marketplace sign-in is stored encrypted, is never shown again, and is never written to logs.
  Crossmart reads only what it needs from orders: what was sold, never who bought it.
</Aside>
